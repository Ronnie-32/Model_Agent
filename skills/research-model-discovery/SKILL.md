# Research and Model Discovery

Read `docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` before searching.

## Trigger

State:

`I need to know ____ because it could change ____.`

If the second blank is not a material architecture, evidence, decision, or citation issue, do not search.

## Work

1. Generate 2–4 serious native candidate directions.
2. Search by geometry, data structure, mechanism/objective/constraint, and validation—not winner keywords.
3. Prefer primary/official sources; use repositories/mirrors only for discovery or artifact access.
4. Verify source identity and supporting content separately. Verify award paper identity against official results.
5. Compare candidates compactly:

`assumptions | structural fit | identification | failure modes | validation | feasibility | source IDs`

6. Update `source_ledger.csv` for every retained external fact/data/method/citation.
7. Record only the modeling consequence and why serious alternatives lost.

## Stop

Stop when the gap is resolved enough to decide, strong alternatives are understood, central claims are verified, and another source is unlikely to change the conclusion.

Do not create a literature essay unless the final deliverable requires one. Do not copy current-contest solution material when rules or integrity prohibit it.
