# Final Audit

## Requirement closure

- Every task verb has an answer, artifact and evidence link.
- Quantities, units, time windows and populations agree across text, tables, figures and code.
- Sub-question outputs form the declared architecture rather than isolated mini-solutions.

## Claim and evidence integrity

- `claim_evidence.csv` contains every headline and decision-critical claim.
- Observed, derived, latent, assumed and decision quantities are labeled correctly.
- No correlation, feature importance or predictive fit is presented as causality.
- “Optimal”, “best” and similar wording is no stronger than the benchmark, feasible set and sensitivity evidence.
- External identities, quotations and bibliographic facts are verified in `source_ledger.csv` when research was used.

## Modeling and decision integrity

- Assumptions are necessary, visible and paired with a check, sensitivity or explicit boundary.
- Constructed metrics have a mechanism or no-effect baseline, scale, direction and validation.
- Hard constraints and support are satisfied in code and reported solutions.
- The dominant failure risk of each central claim has been tested.
- Material uncertainty reaches the downstream comparison, objective, constraint or recommendation.
- The integrated red team found no unresolved blocking defect.

## Reproducibility and communication

- Code, data, random seeds, parameters and outputs reproduce the reported numbers within declared tolerance.
- Figures reveal a decision-relevant relationship and remain readable at submission size.
- Abstract states the problem, architecture, evidence-backed findings, decision value and limitations.
- Paper, appendices and supporting materials obey current competition rules.
- Human review decisions required by the selected review mode are recorded.
